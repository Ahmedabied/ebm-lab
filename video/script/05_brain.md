## p01 | viz: loop diagram, Brain box lit. The need, as one line on screen: "x (12 numbers) → one energy per move". The legend "F energy, low wins | Q = minus F, high wins" sits in a corner for the whole chapter.
Fourth box: the brain. The need: twelve readings in, and one energy for each of the three moves out. We now build the formula that computes it, one symbol at a time, and every symbol gets pointed at while it is named.

## p02 | viz: the full formula appears, dim: F(x,a) = −b_a − w_a·x − Σ_h softplus(c_h + u_h·x + v_{h,a}). Nothing highlighted.
Here it is whole. Do not read it yet. We build it from the inside, and by the end you will be able to read it in one breath.

## p03 | viz: a second line: Q(x,a) = b_a + w_a·x + Σ_h softplus(…), labelled "evidence for move a"; then F = −Q. The price tags: more evidence, lower price.
First, the minus signs. Everything inside the formula is evidence for a move: reasons to take it. Add the evidence up and you have Q. Energy is minus Q. Why flip the sign at all? Convention: we agreed in chapter one that energy is a price and low wins, so a move with a lot of evidence must be cheap. Three minus signs, one meaning. From here I say evidence and Q, and the energy is the same number with the sign flipped.

This brain had no strong habit.

## p07 | viz: the Σ_h softplus(…) term highlighted. A smoke alarm beside it; then inside the term, c_h, u_h·x, v_{h,a} pointed at in turn. A committee of H small boxes, each with twelve input knobs (u), a threshold (c) and three output knobs (v).
Now the committee. A hidden unit is a smoke alarm. It has its own sensitivity to each smell: that is u, twelve knobs, its own weighted sum of the readings. It has a threshold before it speaks at all: that is c, its own resting preference. And it is wired to say something about each move: that is v, three knobs. Add the three parts and you get the unit's input for this move, call it z. The sigma with a little h is the sum over all the units, and each one is a small model inside the model. One difference from a real alarm: a real one clicks on; this one fades in, which is the next word.

## p08 | viz: softplus. The curve max(0, z) in grey; it morphs into log(1 + e^z). Slider on z: at z = −3 output 0.049; z = 0 output 0.693; z = +3 output 3.049. Underneath, a tangent slides along the curve and its slope is plotted as an S curve from 0 to 1: 0.047, 0.5, 0.953, labelled "sigmoid".
Then softplus. Log of one plus e to the z. Look at the shape: for very negative z it is nearly zero, for very positive z it is nearly z itself, and it bends smoothly in between. It is a hinge that says "count it if positive", rounded off. Why a hinge at all, and not just add z? Because a unit that can go quiet is a detector. If every unit always spoke, the committee would be nothing but more linear knobs. Why rounded and not the sharp corner? Because learning needs a slope everywhere. Its slope is a number between zero and one, how switched on the unit is: at minus three, five hundredths; at zero, one half; at plus three, ninety-five hundredths. That slope curve is called the sigmoid, and it comes back in the code.

## p09 | viz: WHERE SOFTPLUS COMES FROM. One unit, two states: off (evidence 0) and on (evidence z). Two tickets, e^0 = 1 and e^z, drawn as bars; they stack into 1 + e^z; log of the stack is the softplus. The word "free" appears. A small note: "inside the committee T is fixed at one".
Here is where that exact curve comes from, and it is the reason this is called a free energy. Imagine the unit can be on or off. Off contributes no evidence. On contributes z. We do not decide which. We count the tickets for both, the way softmax does, with the temperature fixed at one inside the committee: e to the zero, which is one, plus e to the z. The log of that total is log of one plus e to the z. Softplus. "Free" means: the unit's on-or-off was summed out, so the formula is free of it. That is the whole mystery of the word.

## p14 | viz: SYMMETRY. The twelve readings in two groups: the three contrasts (flip sign if the antennae swap) and the nine others (identical for both antennae). Q_LEFT − Q_RIGHT for seed 203 split into the contrast part and the rest, as two bars moving over a day.
One honest note on this brain. Only the three contrast readings know left from right. Every other reading, the means, the changes, the body, the constant one, is the same for both antennae, noise included, so their knobs can only add a turning bias that does not know which side. The left-minus-right information lives in the three contrast knobs.

## p16 | viz: the formula read in one breath, each piece lighting up as spoken; then the loop diagram with the Brain box filled: "x → F(x,·), three numbers".
Read it in one breath. Energy of move a is minus the resting preference, minus the move's weighted readings, minus the committee's rounded votes. Q is the same with the signs flipped. Act on the highest Q. Stripped down: evidence is a weighted sum plus a committee, energy is its negative, and the choice is the sign of a difference.
